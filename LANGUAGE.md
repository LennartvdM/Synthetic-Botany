# Visual language: a notebook

Terms noticed in the reference pictures shared so far. This is a list of
observations, kept open on purpose. It ranks nothing, concludes nothing and
plans nothing. Add to it; do not tidy it into a system.

## From the flower photographs

- A wall whose angle changes more than once along its length: opens fast,
  runs parallel, opens again at the end.
- A rim that turns outward without breaking into folds.
- A mouth cut at a slant, one side longer than the other.
- Bodies overlapping like tiles, each pressed under the one above.
- Size and openness changing steadily along a row, closed at one end.
- Mouths packing into a surface.

## From the bulb pictures

- Thick plates wrapped over a core, standing off it, with crescent gaps.
- Slits that show a second body inside the first.
- A globe divided along its meridians.
- A body held in a crown of prongs with knobbed ends.
- A star of pointed tongues draped over the top of a globe.
- Small spheres gathered round a base.
- A tuft of fat tapering fingers from one point.
- Volume everywhere; nothing thin.

## From the concept sheet (six colour panels, eight sketches)

- One kind of elongated part, repeated and packed round an axis. The part
  changes between panels; the packing does not.
- Parts ending at different heights, so a column's surface is made of tips.
- The tip carrying the character of the part: open mouth with a pale rim,
  pale pad, wavy point, flat pointed scale, bead.
- A base zone where the same parts shorten and splay into a skirt.
- The same family of part shown twice in one picture: packed side by side
  in the large body, radiating from a point in the small one.
- One thin curling line joining a small form to a large one; thick against
  thin.
- A few thick trunks twisted round each other, straps peeling away.
- Hanging from above.
- Features on one side only: a row of pegs down one flank, a comb of teeth
  along an inner curve.
- A hood with an opening. A pair of long legs from a knot. A veil of fine
  fibres. A string of scales. A large pod in a nest of pointed bracts.

## Words used for the feeling

More fantastic. More volume. Not noisy. Substance. Deft.

## Recurring across more than one source

- Bundling: many parts sharing an axis (the cord of strands from the first
  renders; the packed columns).
- Parts that stand off each other so the gap between them reads.
- A different treatment at the tip or rim than along the length.
